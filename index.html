About

Aarush Das is a BBA student specialising in Strategy and Business Analytics at CHRIST (Deemed to be University), Bannerghatta Road Campus. He is also enrolled in the Digital Business and Entrepreneurship (DBE) programme at IIM Bangalore.

Drawn to business strategy, analytics, entrepreneurship and leadership, Aarush is developing a sharp understanding of how businesses work and the skills needed to lead them. Teamwork, coordination, problem-solving and continuous learning shape the way he works.

Beyond the classroom, Aarush contributes to social initiatives, competes in debates, and has won multiple inter-house football and basketball competitions. He is the co-author of the published book Those 365 Days of My Life. His long-term goal is to become a strategic, analytical and entrepreneurial professional who creates meaningful impact.
